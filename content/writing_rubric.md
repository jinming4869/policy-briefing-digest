# Writing Rubric

## Facts
Identify title, publisher, date, document type, policy object, effective date/deadline, and official source.

## Column Fit
See SKILL.md §4.

## Business Relevance
Each entry must connect to at least one game/Internet scenario.

## Short Version (正式版) Checklist
- [ ] One key point per item
- [ ] No generic "企业应关注"
- [ ] No AI self-correction ("本事项属于……不宜……")
- [ ] Legal citation: hyperlink + natural embedding, no article numbers
- [ ] Compliance change: describe actual benefit, not label change
- [ ] Game company advice: single thrust only
- [ ] News section: facts only (who, when, what, how many, deadline). No trend analysis or rhythm comparison.
- [ ] Interpretation section: analysis only. No re-stating facts from news section.
- [ ] News section information density: list specific violation types when available, not just "covering multiple scenarios"
